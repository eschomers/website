Trio KRÆKK is an ensemble focusing on contemporary and experimental music, consisting of Maren Sofie Nyland Johansen, Tomas Laukvik Nannestad and Elias Nurmi Schomers. In addition to their respective instruments accordion, guitar/lute and cello, the three musicians often play music that makes use of other instruments, everyday objects and more.
